Privacy Policy

Takbir-al-Ula — an FZ Screens product · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Takbir-al-Ula ("the app") displays mosque prayer (salah) and jamaah times, nearby mosques on a map, and community announcements. This policy explains what data the app and its backend service (takbir-al-ula.com / fzscreens.com) handle.

Data we collect

  • Location (optional, on demand) — only when you tap "Near Me", your device location is used to center the map on nearby mosques. Location is used for the lookup and is not stored or associated with your identity.
  • Push notification token — if notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues the device a token so the server can notify displays when their content or prayer times change. The token identifies the device installation, not a person.
  • Account data (mosque administrators only) — mosque owners who manage displays create an account with an email address and password on the website. App viewers do not need an account.
  • Uploaded content — administrators upload images, videos, and prayer schedules; these are stored to serve their displays.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No contact lists, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications (data-only) to displays.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — the map requests tiles from standard tile servers, which receive the device's IP address and requested tile coordinates like any web request.
  • Hosting (Render) — the backend runs on Render's infrastructure; server logs may contain IP addresses and user agents as part of normal operation.

Data retention and account deletion

Administrator accounts and their uploaded content are retained until the administrator deletes them or requests deletion. Device push tokens are removed when the device is unlinked or the token becomes invalid.

To request deletion of your account and associated data:

  • Email growwithfzdigitals@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”, sent from the email address registered to your account.
  • Include your username (and mosque name, if you manage one).
  • We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

Deleted: your account credentials, mosque listing(s), prayer times, uploaded media, linked displays, and their push tokens.

Kept: server access logs (IP address, user agent) are retained for up to 90 days for security and abuse prevention, then purged. Nothing else is kept.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
